## Description

This presentation took place at the annual reserach day organized by the [Vlaamse Vereniging voor Algemene en Vergelijkende Literatuurwetenschap](www.vval.be) (VAL; or 'Flemish Association for General and Comparative Literature Studies'). Flemish universities take turns to organise these events and invite especially early-carreer researchers and newly established research groups across Flanders to present their research to their peers. 

This specific event was organised at the University of Antwerp, and gave my colleagues and me the opportunity to present the [platformDH](https://platformdh.uantwerpen.be) – an informal platform aggregating Digital Humanities research, tools, etc. developed at the University of Antwerp's Department of Literature, and organizing a lecture series. My co-presenter Elli was organizing the lecture series at the time, a role she passed on to me in 2017, and which I in turn passed on to Lamyk Bekius in 2021. The presentation started with a general introduction to Digital Humanities to an audience of researchers in the field of Literary Studies, and ended with a warm invitation to the second edition of the [DHBenelux](www.dhbenelux.org) conference we were organising later that year. 

My part in this presentation focussed on a short presentation of the [Lexicon of Scholarly Editing](https://lexiconse.uantwerpen.be) I was (and still am) developing, as an example of a digital resource that might be relevant to members in the audience.

As far as I know, there exists no abstract for this presentation, and I do not have the rights to share the presentation's slides publicly.
