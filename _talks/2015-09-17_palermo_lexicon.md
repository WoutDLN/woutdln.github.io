## Abstract

The [Lexicon of Scholarly Editing](https://lexiconse.uantwerpen.be)[^1] is an open access academic resource that offers definitions for contested concepts in the field of Scholarly Editing and Textual Criticism. Rather than writing new definitions for theoretical concepts, however, the Lexicon collects and quotes existing definitions, and lets those definitions speak for themselves. To achieve this goal, the definitions of each concept are ordered chronologically, and rendered in their original language. As such, the Lexicon aims to display these definitions as if in a multilingual discussion with one another – which, in some cases, they quite literally are.

Initiated by Dirk Van Hulle, the Lexicon was developed by Wout Dillen as part of the ERC project 'Creative Undoing and Textual Scholarship' (CUTS), and affiliated to the Centre for Manuscript Genetics (CMG) at the University of Antwerp, and the European Society of Textual Scholarship (ESTS). This paper will present the Lexicon and situate it in the multilingual tradition of Textual Criticism, with a special focus on its recent alliance with the FonteGaia project, initiated to increase its share of Italian definitions, and to bridge the gap between Italian Textual Scholarship and other approaches to Textual Criticism.
