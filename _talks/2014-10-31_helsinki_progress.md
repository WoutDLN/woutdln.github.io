### Abstract

This presentation offers the community of the European Society for Textual Scholarship a progress report of the work that has been done on the Lexicon of Scholarly Editing ([www.uantwerpen.be/lexicon-scholarly-editing]([www.uantwerpen.be/lexicon-scholarly-editing)) in the year since its launch at the previous ESTS conference in Paris, France. The Lexicon is an open access academic resource that offers definitions for contested concepts in the field of Scholarly Editing and Textual Criticism. Rather than writing new definitions for these concepts, the Lexicon quotes definitions from academic journals, monographs and the like. As such, the Lexicon aims to reveal the lively multilingual debate these concepts have spurred in the field. Originating from a series of interesting discussions on the need for a such a Lexicon at the annual ESTS meetings, the Lexicon was developed by the University of Antwerp’s Centre for Manuscript Genetics as part of the ERC project titled 'Creative Undoing and Textual Scholarship' (CUTS).
